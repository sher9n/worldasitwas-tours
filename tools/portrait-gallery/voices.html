Assunta Belli · Seamstress · Rome, 1870

Which of these should she be?

Six Italian women, none of them a voice any other guide uses. She was reading in a stock English voice, which is why she sounded British. All six read the same opening line so you can compare like for like.

“Morning to you. I'm Assunta Belli, twenty-nine, a seamstress from Regola, and I sew out of a room near the river for shops and private houses both. I cross these streets with bundles on my arm from one opinion to the other, so I talk piano in public. Still, I saw the breach at Porta Pia.” Her own words · the Italian in them is what exposes a false accent
Violetta is recorded and live now. If you prefer one of the others, tell me and I will re-record her whole walk in it; it takes about a minute and costs around a dollar.
